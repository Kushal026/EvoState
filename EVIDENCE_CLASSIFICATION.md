## 🏷️ 1. The 4-Tier Evidence Taxonomy

Every scientific claim, architectural property, benchmark number, and interactive visualization in EvoState is explicitly tagged with one of the following four evidence classes:

| Tier Badge | Badge Name | Definition & Verification Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `[PUBLISHED RESULT]` | **Published Result** | Peer-reviewed or established preprint empirical data and theoretical proofs directly cited from foundational literature (e.g. Sun et al., 2025; Gu & Dao, 2023; Snell et al., 2024). |
| `[INDEPENDENT RESULT]` | **Independent Result** | Third-party replications, open-source implementations, or classical mathematical theorems (e.g. Hopfield 1982 capacity limit $\alpha_c \approx 0.14$, Johnson-Lindenstrauss lemma). |
| `[OUR EXPERIMENT]` | **Our Experiment** | Deterministic empirical sweeps generated by our PyTorch engine (2,700 trials across 50 seeds in `data/precomputed/sweeps_master.csv`) and live FastAPI runs. |
| `[EDUCATIONAL SIMPLIFICATION]` | **Educational Simplification** | Pedagogical models, isolated $d=32$ surrogates, and interactive D3 animations engineered to convey intuition without claiming production equivalence. |

## 📋 2. Comprehensive Claim & Evidence Mapping

### A. Central Project Claim
> *"A fixed-size evolving state can carry useful information across sequences without storing every previous token, but increasing sequence length and conflicting updates can cause interference and information loss; additional inference-time computation can sometimes improve recovery."*

- **Classification:** `[OUR EXPERIMENT]` (Verified via 2,700 seeded trials in `data/precomputed/`) & `[PUBLISHED RESULT]` (Theoretically proven in Sun et al., 2025 and Snell et al., 2024).

### B. Core Architecture Claims

| Claim / Mechanism | Evidence Tier | Primary Source & Empirical Verification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Attention as Associative Memory Retrieval:** Transformer self-attention can be mathematically formulated as associative matrix readouts. | `[PUBLISHED RESULT]` | Sun et al. (2025), Section 2; Ramsauer et al. (2020). |

| **Inference-Time Scaling (BDH-CQ Energy Settling):** Iterative query sharpening ($C_{\text{infer}} > 1$) can resolve superposition noise without updating model parameters. | `[OUR EXPERIMENT]` & `[PUBLISHED RESULT]` | Snell et al. (2024); Experiment 5: Inference Recovery sweep. |

## 🚫 3. Prohibited Scientific Practices
1. **Never Fake Live Computation:** If the FastAPI backend or client simulator is executing, it is badged as `LIVE EXPERIMENT`. Heavy precomputed sweeps are badged as `PRECOMPUTED EXPERIMENT`.
2. **Never Fabricate Numbers:** All summary statistics and plot lines match the exact means and standard errors computed from `sweeps_master.csv`.
3. **Never Force Claim Verdicts:** The 60-second learning test dynamically grades the learner's actual experimental outcomes against ground truth, yielding `SUPPORTED`, `CHALLENGED`, or `INCONCLUSIVE`.
